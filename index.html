03Approach

Integrate, don't replace.

We don't replace the systems you already run. We build technology that works with them, for hospitals, clinics, and businesses alike.

01

Integrate

We connect to your existing systems, from hospital HMIS and EMR to business tools, through APIs, HL7, and FHIR.

02

Protect

Consent before any recording or sharing. Encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, audit trails, and de-identified data for analytics.

03

Co-design

We build each tool with the people who will use it every day, from doctors and nurses to operations teams.

Designed to align with

  • DPDP Act 2023
  • ABDM
  • HL7 FHIR R4
  • SNOMED CT
  • NABH Digital Health Standards
